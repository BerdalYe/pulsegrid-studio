## What it does
- **Make music your way:** channel rack, patterns, piano roll, playlist and mixer.
- **Your music is yours:** free to use, and you own everything you make, commercial releases included.
- **Cloud sync (optional):** sign in and your projects follow you between PCs and the web studio. Without an account, nothing leaves your computer.
- **Keeps itself up to date:** the Windows app downloads updates in the background.
- **Fast to learn:** a short first-run tour and a full shortcut list on **F1**. The scroll wheel zooms the piano roll and playlist.

## Install
1. Download `PulseGrid-Studio-Setup-1.0.0.exe` from the [latest release](https://github.com/BerdalYe/pulsegrid-studio/releases/latest).
2. Run it. The installer isn't code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn: choose **More info → Run anyway**.
